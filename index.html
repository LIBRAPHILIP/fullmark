What the contract will and will not do

The desk is a window. These rules are the ones in contracts/fullmark.py.

Contract-built URL

Claim takes a record id. The contract fetches https://pointercrate.com/api/v1/records/<id>/. The slash is part of the path. A sponsor cannot pass a URL.

Ids, not names

Player id, demon id, progress, status, the banned flag, and the completion timestamp decide the purse. Names and list positions do not. Positions move while a bounty is open.

Approved, or nothing

Status must be approved, progress must meet the bar, and the player must not be banned. A pending or rejected record leaves the bounty open.

Fresh window

A standing prize accepts any approved record. A fresh prize requires the completion timestamp on the record to be on or after the open. The host clock is not used.

Fail closed

An unreadable body, a 404, or a Cloudflare page does not pay and does not refund early. After the deadline the sponsor withdraws.

Unwind

Sponsor and payee can both consent before the deadline. One signature is not enough, unless they are the same account.
